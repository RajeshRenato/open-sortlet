    The real app, sorting a morning of mail from two inboxes.

    Sorted into the categories you choose.

    Every email lands in exactly one place. Sortlet starts with ten categories that fit most inboxes; rename them, add your own or remove them in Settings → Categories, one per line.

    A typical week of mail, as it fills the "Sorted into" rack in the sidebar.

    Pick what does the sorting

    Claude Code (local)

    Uses your installed claude CLI and your own Claude login. No API key needed.

    Claude API

    Uses an Anthropic API key. Most reliable for large scans.

    Rules only

    Free and offline keyword and sender rules. Nothing leaves your computer.

    Your choices stick. Move an email to another category by hand and re-sorting never moves it back. If an AI batch fails, the rules step in so nothing stays unsorted.

    Two flags for what's waiting on you.

    Needs reply

    A real person asked you something and is waiting on your answer. Newsletters and no-reply senders never land here.

    Needs attention

    Something to act on even when there's nothing to reply to: failed payments, expiring cards, new sign-ins, changed flights, signatures waiting. Each one says why.

    Mark done

    Handled it? Mark it done in the reading pane, in chat, or from Claude, and it leaves both lists.

      Your mail stays on your computer.

      There's no Sortlet server and no account to create. The app talks to your mail provider and, for sorting and chat, to Claude. That's all.

      Stored locally

      Emails live in a database on your machine that only your user account can read.

      Encrypted secrets

      Passwords, sign-in tokens and API keys are encrypted with your system keychain.

      Deletes on a schedule

      Keep everything, or remove stored emails after 7 days to a year. Your mailbox is never touched.

      Can't send mail

      Gmail access is read plus drafts. Sortlet can't send, delete or move your mail.

      Offline option

      Choose the rules engine and sorting never leaves your computer.

      Updates itself

      Sortlet checks for new versions at startup and every few hours, and updates itself.

      Install it in a minute.

      Version 0.2.0. Pick your system; the steps cover the one-time security prompt for apps downloaded outside an app store.

      1. Download the .dmg for your Mac, open it, and drag Sortlet to Applications.
      2. Open Sortlet. macOS blocks it the first time because the app isn't notarized yet.
      3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then Open. You only do this once.
      4. Add an account: Sign in with Google for Gmail, or pick your provider and use an app password.
